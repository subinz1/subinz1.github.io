How I work

Reliability is not a status. It is a chain of evidence.

I work at the boundary between an upstream project and the systems that must keep pace with it. The goal is not just to run a job: preserve its identity, prove what was tested, and let the result reach the person who can act.

  1. 01Capture change contextrevision, event, scope
  2. 02Validate in the right targethardware, OS, build
  3. 03Keep the final result connectedattempt, artifact, report
  4. 04Bring evidence to the decisionHUD, review, release
